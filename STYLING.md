# Slate Club styling

style.css loads three presentation layers:
- styles/slate-club.css: palette, typography and radius tokens.
- styles/layout.css: page structure and responsive grids.
- styles/components.css: controls, exercise cards, progress and state appearance.

Workout behavior, storage and plan data remain in app.js and data.json.
The browser toolbar color in index.html matches the page surface token.
The design/ui-ux prototype retains its separate flow and migration work.
